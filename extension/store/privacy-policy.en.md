## What the extension does

The Vosio extension records the audio of the online meeting in the current browser tab together with your microphone and uploads the recording to **your own Vosio installation** for transcription. Recording starts only through your action (clicking the icon, a keyboard shortcut, or the button in the extension window).

## Data processed

- **Meeting and microphone audio** – stored continuously on your computer in browser storage (IndexedDB) while recording. After the upload to Vosio is confirmed, the local copy is deleted. You can download or delete unsent recordings in the extension window at any time.
- **Pairing data** – the address of your Vosio, your account e-mail and OAuth tokens (narrow `vosio:capture` permission: create new recordings, upload audio, start transcription; it cannot read existing recordings). Stored locally in the browser only. The extension never sees your password – sign-in happens on your Vosio's own page.
- **Settings** – mode, custom sites, Vosio address; stored in synced browser storage (according to your sync settings).
- **URL and title of the active tab** – read locally only to recognise a meeting and to name the recording; not sent anywhere except as the recording title.

## Who receives the data

Only the Vosio installation you paired yourself (its web application and its storage). The extension publisher receives none of your data, uses no analytics or advertising, and neither sells nor shares data with third parties. Processing of recordings and transcripts inside your Vosio is governed by your own configuration and by the transcription provider you use there (you are the controller of that data).

## Recording personal communication – your responsibility

Meeting audio may contain personal data of other people. Inform participants before recording and ensure a legal basis (for example consent). The extension reminds you before the first recording.

## Your choices

- Disconnect at any time in the extension settings or in Vosio (Settings → Connected apps); access is revoked immediately.
- Uninstalling the extension removes all local extension data.
- A pairing lasts at most 30 days, after which you sign in again.
